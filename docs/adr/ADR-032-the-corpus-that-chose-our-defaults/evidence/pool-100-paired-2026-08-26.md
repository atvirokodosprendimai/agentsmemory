# Trial 2 — the pool-width test, paired at n=54 — 2026-08-26

The SAME 54 cases, the same gold, the same commit and ranking configuration as
`real-corpus-large.md`. Only `--pool` changed, 30 → 100. A properly paired comparison.

**This test was pre-specified with both outcomes named, by the n=54 run's own output:**

> "Raise `--pool` and re-run: if they come back, the ranking is fine and the pool was
> too small; if they stay missing, the embedding is not placing those memories near
> their question."

## Result: they came back. The pool was too small.

| | pool 30 | pool 100 |
|---|---|---|
| in pool | 94% | **100%** |
| top-50 | 94% | 94% |
| answers no arm retrieved | **3** | **0** |

The three sit between rank 50 and 100. The embedding places them near their question;
nothing was fetching far enough. **Not a corpus problem, not an embedding problem — a
fetch-width one.**

## And every arm gained from it, except the one we ship

| arm | pool 30 | pool 100 | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vector` | 0.587 | 0.587 | 0.000 |
| `rrf` | 0.636 | 0.645 | +0.009 |
| `hybrid` | 0.647 | **0.697** | +0.050 |
| `fusion bm25=0.60 anchored:ceiling` | 0.650 | **0.705** | +0.055 |
| `rrf+rerank norm=sigmoid` | 0.666 | 0.687 | +0.021 |
| `rerank blend w=0.25` | 0.694 | **0.730** (best) | +0.036 |
| **`production (Search)`** | **0.660, 8 missed** | **0.660, 8 missed** | **0.000** |

**`production` did not move, and that is the finding.** Every other arm re-orders the
shared retrieved pool, so widening it lifts them. `Search` performs its OWN retrieval —
`candidateKFor(limit, …)` is `limit×3`, raised to `RERANK_POOL` when a reranker is
configured — so `--pool` cannot reach it. It is structurally excluded from a gain of
~0.05 MRR that every other arm collected.

Its 8 misses are not pool misses: the pool is 100% complete at pool 100 and the arms
report 0 unreachable. They are golds RETRIEVED AND RANKED, then cut by the page. The
run says so, and the table prices the alternatives:

| production variant | MRR | missed |
|---|---|---|
| `production (Search)` | 0.660 | 8 |
| `production (Search) limit=10` | 0.665 | **5** |
| `production (Search) retrieve-k=50` | 0.667 | 6 |

Raising `limit` from 5 to 10 removes three of the eight. Neither variant reaches
`hybrid`'s 0.697, so the page cut is not the whole story — but it is the only knob here
that moves the miss count.

## A caveat this trial forces onto an earlier finding

**The closet prior's measured cost was substantially an artifact of a narrow pool.**

| corpus | pool | ΔMRR | 95% paired CI | Δrecall@1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| paraphrase n=30 | 30 | −0.048 | [−0.11, −0.00] | −0.067 |
| real n=26 | 30 | −0.039 | [−0.10, 0.00] | −0.077 |
| real n=54 | 30 | −0.027 | [−0.06, −0.00] | −0.039 |
| **real n=54** | **100** | **−0.002** | **[−0.01, −0.00]** | **+0.000** |

Still negative, still excluding zero, and now numerically negligible — recall@1 is
exactly unchanged. Three runs agreeing on a cost is weaker evidence than it looked,
because all three shared a pool width. It remains `closet-boost=0.00` in production and
there is still no case for turning it on; the honest statement is "it does nothing
measurable at a healthy pool width", not "it costs recall".

## What this trial does NOT settle

- `rrf` reads "worse by 0.01–0.16" here, having been inconclusive at pool 30. That is a
  SECOND LOOK at a hypothesis this ADR pre-registered against chasing. The pool change
  was made for a different, pre-specified reason and this fell out of it. The defensible
  number is the gap — `hybrid` 0.697 and `ceiling` 0.705 against `rrf` 0.645 — not the
  "vs best" verdict, whose baseline is selected from its own table.
- Gold is still judged by a 7B model, so absolute figures remain judge-limited.
- Pool 100 is not free: it is more rows hydrated and a larger candidate set per query.
  This trial measures QUALITY at that width and says nothing about its latency cost.

## Addendum 2026-09-08 — the in-table `anchored:ceiling` row was read as a shipping result, and it cannot be one

Appended, not edited: every number above is correct and none of it is withdrawn. What
this addendum records is a MISREADING of it, because the same misreading is available to
the next person who opens this file.

A session quoted `fusion bm25=0.60 anchored:ceiling` **0.705** against `rrf` **0.645**
from the table above and concluded the shipped fusion default was measurably the worse
choice. That conclusion was wrong, and this file already said why — two sections up:

> `production` did not move, and that is the finding. Every other arm re-orders the
> shared retrieved pool … It is structurally excluded from a gain of ~0.05 MRR that
> every other arm collected.

The number was quoted; the sentence beside it was read past.

**A pre-registered A/B on 2026-09-08 measured the size of the error.** Two runs differing
only in `--fusion`, on a different palace (3,390 drawers, v0.0.128, qdrant, **no
reranker**), judge held constant:

| | MRR |
|---|---|
| in-table `fusion bm25=0.60 anchored:ceiling`, run A (`--fusion rrf`) | 0.550 |
| in-table `fusion bm25=0.60 anchored:ceiling`, run B (`--fusion linear …`) | **0.550** |
| `production (Search)` with that config actually shipped, run B | **0.433** |
| `production (Search)` shipped default, run A | **0.521** |

All 24 pool-scoped arms were **byte-identical between the two runs**. The in-table arm
does not respond to `--fusion` at all, and overstates the challenger by ~0.12 MRR. Shipped
for real, the challenger LOST by 0.088. Verdict was `NO CHANGE`; `rrf` stays.

⚠ **So no in-table row in this file predicts what shipping that configuration does.** They
are re-orderings of one shared candidate pool. Only a `production (Search)` row, compared
against another run's `production (Search)` row, answers a shipping question — which is
what ADR-032's own Decision meant by refusing to flip on this table.

Scope: that A/B ran with **no reranker**, on a corpus unlike this one, judged by a 3B
model. Deltas hold (judge identical across arms); absolute MRR does not. Whether
`linear + ceiling` wins with a cross-encoder in front of it is untested.
